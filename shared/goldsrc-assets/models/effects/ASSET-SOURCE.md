# Original shell models

`rshell.mdl` and `pshell.mdl` are unmodified Valve assets from Steam App 90's
`cstrike/models` directory, downloaded and validated on 2026-07-26.

```text
rshell.mdl  6775dfe6c227b66d7f3f171b1066c6c0dbd7d6f64878021d7d68da2b62ed2e61
pshell.mdl  5cff2c288600c5861e1fd84b64b7c9964327a4aa796ed1a578bf965d0f7691cc
```

They are converted by `scripts/import-game-vfx.sh` through the repository's
existing audited GoldSrc MDL converter.
