# Classic Counter-Strike models

This bundle contains the 179 `.mdl` files from Valve's Counter-Strike base
content distributed through SteamCMD/HLDS App 90. They were imported from the
validated `cstrike/models` depot on 2026-07-21.

The runtime layout is organized by use:

- `player/` — the nine stock player models
- `weapons/` — stock carried, view, dropped, and shield weapon models
- `classic/` — hostages, equipment effects, and stock map props

These original game assets remain Valve content. Distribution and use must
comply with the applicable Steam Subscriber Agreement and Valve terms.
